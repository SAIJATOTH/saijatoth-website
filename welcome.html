Why I'm starting this blog

A space to write about research, markets, the economy, world events and life, in plain language.

Dr. Jatoth Sai Kiran10 Oct 20261 min read

Most of my research ends up in journals, conference halls and classrooms. This blog is a place to write more freely, and about much more than my research.

What to expect

There's no fixed beat here. I'll write about whatever I'm thinking about: my research, what's happening in markets and the economy, news from India and around the world, and things from everyday life. Some posts will be quick reactions to a current event. Others will be longer pieces that take an idea apart and look at what it means for people.

Expect a mix of:

  • Research: ideas and findings from marketing, consumer behaviour and neuromarketing.
  • Markets and the economy: what's changing in business, prices, technology and the way people spend.
  • The world around us: issues and events from India and beyond, and what they tell us.
  • Life: personal notes, lessons from teaching, and whatever else seems worth sharing.

An open conversation

These posts are my own views, written to start a conversation. If something here is useful, or you see it differently, I'd be glad to hear from you at jatothsai@gmail.com.
